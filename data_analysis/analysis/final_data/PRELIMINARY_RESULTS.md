# Preliminary Results

N = 917 (of 925; 8 excluded). Five conditions, 12 four-option planning problems.

## Performance

| Condition | n | Score M (SD) | Post-estimate M (SD) | Estimation error M (SD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI (baseline) | 187 | 5.57 (1.81) | 9.70 (2.37) | 4.42 (2.42) |
| Reliability cards | 182 | 5.18 (1.63) | 8.23 (2.14) | 3.35 (2.23) |
| Alternatives | 181 | 5.20 (1.53) | 7.70 (2.22) | 2.96 (2.01) |
| Pause points | 184 | 4.49 (2.00) | 7.77 (2.56) | 3.61 (2.45) |
| Reflection task | 183 | 5.52 (1.65) | 9.15 (2.54) | 4.03 (2.48) |

All participants substantially overestimate their performance. No intervention improves actual scores; pause points lowers them (g = -0.57).

## Hypothesis tests (directional, Holm-corrected)

Each intervention vs AI baseline. Supported = p_holm < .05 in the predicted direction.

| Intervention | Estimation error | Confidence discrimination | Actual score |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reliability cards | **g = -0.46, p < .001** | **g = +0.50, p < .001** | not supported |
| Alternatives | **g = -0.65, p < .001** | **g = +0.28, p = .011** | not supported |
| Pause points | **g = -0.33, p = .002** | not supported | not supported |
| Reflection task | not supported (p = .064) | not supported | not supported |

## Verdict

| Intervention | Hypothesis supported? | Via |
|---|---|---|
| Reliability cards | Yes | metacognition (lower error + better discrimination) |
| Alternatives | Yes | metacognition (lower error + better discrimination) |
| Pause points | Partial | metacognition (lower error only) |
| Reflection task | No | — |

## Mechanism

The metacognitive gains come primarily from **lowered self-estimates** (post-study estimates drop 1.5-2.0 problems in the effective interventions vs baseline), not from improved performance. Reliability cards and alternatives additionally sharpen item-level confidence discrimination — participants become better at being more confident when correct and less confident when incorrect.

## User experience cost

Every intervention worsens user experience relative to baseline:

| Scale | Baseline | Reliability | Alternatives | Pause pts | Reflection |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SUS (0-100) | 79.5 | 74.6 | 67.5 | 59.9 | 72.8 |
| Trust (1-5) | 3.78 | 3.45 | 3.15 | 3.26 | 3.64 |
| NASA-TLX (1-20) | 7.67 | 9.22 | 9.52 | 9.67 | 11.68 |
| UEQ-S (-3 to +3) | 1.31 | 1.07 | 0.80 | 0.21 | 0.86 |

Pause points is the costliest (lowest SUS, lowest UEQ, the only condition that also harms performance). Reflection task adds the most workload and roughly doubles completion time but preserves trust best among the interventions.

## Caveat

Wave 1 collected baseline + reliability cards (Jul 2026); wave 2 collected alternatives + pause points + reflection task (Aug 2026). Cross-wave comparisons carry a cohort confound. Within-wave-2 sensitivity analyses confirm the pause-points accuracy cost and the alternatives metacognition advantage hold without the confound.
