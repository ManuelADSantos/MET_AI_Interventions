# Study Information

## General Information

> Thank you for your interest in this study.

> In this study, you will answer a set of planning and organizing questions while working together with an AI assistant. The goal of the study is to better understand how people use AI support when solving reasoning and decision-making tasks.

> The study will take approximately 40-60 minutes. Your participation is voluntary. You may stop participating at any time by closing the study page. If you stop before completing the study, your responses may not be included in the analysis.

> There are no known risks associated with participating in this study beyond those normally involved in using a computer.


## Privacy

> During the study, we will collect your answers, your confidence ratings, and your interactions with the AI assistant. This may include the prompts you enter and the responses generated by the AI assistant.

> The data will be analyzed for scientific research purposes only. Your responses will be treated confidentially and stored securely. We will not publish any information that directly identifies you.

> Please do not enter any personal or sensitive information into the AI assistant during the study.

> Your data will be processed in accordance with applicable data protection regulations. If you have questions about the study or your data, you can contact:

> Dr. Robin Welsch
> Institution: Aalto University, School of Science, Department of Computer Science
> E-mail: robin.welsch@aalto.fi
